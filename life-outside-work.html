Outside The Office

What I'm into when I'm not building roadmaps.

Client work is where most of my week goes — but a fair bit of personal time goes into following markets for the sake of it, and thinking through money in plain, everyday terms. Here's a bit more of that side of things.

INTERESTS

What I follow

Markets don't switch off when the workday ends, and honestly, I don't want them to. I spend a lot of personal time reading into macro trends and how they filter down to individual sectors and names — partly because it sharpens the advice I give clients, and partly because I just find it interesting.

The other half is personal finance at the everyday level — less about markets, more about the small habits and decisions that quietly compound (or quietly wreck) someone's financial position over the years.

And increasingly, I'm interested in using data tools and AI to build things faster — not as a hobby separate from finance, but as a way of shipping ideas (content, research, tools) that would otherwise stay stuck as drafts.
